Fast Pet Safety Checklists

Key takeaways every pet owner should pin on their fridge.

Toxic Food Alert

Never feed chocolate, onions, grapes, raisins, xylitol sweetener, or cooked bones to pets.

Hydration Target

Dogs need roughly 50-60ml of fresh water per kilogram of body weight each day.

Pavement Heat Rule

If asphalt is too hot to hold your bare hand for 7 seconds, it will burn your dog's paws.

Vaccine Timing

Annual booster vaccinations protect your companion against virulent infectious diseases.
