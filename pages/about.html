Our Origins

Redefining Compassionate Pet Healthcare in the UK

Founded in 2014 by a dedicated team of veterinary surgeons and animal lovers in London, Pet Care Services began with a singular vision: to create a holistic animal care sanctuary where clinical medical excellence meets warmth, respect, and unconditional love.

Over the past decade, we have expanded to provide comprehensive veterinary surgical care, certified Fear-Free grooming spas, positive behavioral puppy schools, and carefully formulated organic nutrition—all under one loving roof.
