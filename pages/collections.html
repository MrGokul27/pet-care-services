Clinical Cleanliness

Uncompromising Safety & Hygiene Standards

We uphold rigorous medical sanitation standards across every grooming station, exam table, and hotel room.

Medical-Grade Autoclave Sterilization

Every scissor, blade, clipper, and surgical instrument undergoes high-temperature steam sterilization after every single patient.

Non-Toxic Botanical Sanitation

Our cleaning solutions are 100% pet-safe, veterinary disinfectant certified to neutralize parvovirus, kennel cough, and bacteria within seconds.

No Cage-Drying Policy

We never use dangerous heated cage dryers. All pets are hand-dried with gentle warm airflow by dedicated groomers to avoid overheating.

Continuous Vital Monitoring

During any sedative or anaesthetic procedure, blood oxygen, respiratory rate, and blood pressure are digitally monitored by a registered veterinary nurse.
