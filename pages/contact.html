Urgent Guide

What to Do in a Pet Medical Emergency

Follow these vital steps while heading to our Kensington hospital.

01

Call +91 9876543210 First

Calling in advance allows our surgical and triage team to prepare the emergency room, oxygen masks, and IV catheters before your vehicle arrives.

02

Keep Your Pet Warm & Calm

Wrap your pet in a clean, soft blanket. Minimize sudden movements and do not administer human medications (such as paracetamol/ibuprofen), which are toxic to pets.

03

Bring Any Ingested Toxins

If your pet swallowed a chemical, plant, or packaging, bring the container with you to help our toxicologists identify the exact antidote quickly.
