Urgent Guide

What to Do in a Pet Medical Emergency

Follow these vital protocol steps while heading to our Kensington hospital.

01

Call Hotline First

Calling ahead allows our surgical and critical care team to prepare oxygen incubators, IV lines, and trauma suites before you arrive.

+91 9876543210
02

Keep Warm & Calm

Wrap your pet gently in a clean blanket to combat shock. Minimize sudden motion and never give human painkillers (paracetamol/ibuprofen).

Stabilize Body Heat
03

Bring Ingested Toxins

If your pet swallowed a chemical, toxic plant, or foreign packaging, bring the label or sample so our toxicologists match the exact antidote.

Bring Packaging / Plants
